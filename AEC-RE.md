# AEC in Real Estate (AEC RE)

AEC stands for **Architecture, Engineering and Construction**. In real estate, AEC RE covers the professional work that turns land and a brief into buildings that can be sold, leased or operated.

## Scope

- **Architecture**: concept design, space planning, building layout, zoning and planning compliance, and design coordination.
- **Engineering**: structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire-safety systems, plus sustainability and energy performance.
- **Construction**: cost estimating, procurement, contract management, scheduling, site supervision and handover.
- **Real estate**: feasibility studies, site selection, development budgets, financing, sales or leasing, and asset management after completion.

## Typical project stages

1. Feasibility and site appraisal
2. Concept and schematic design
3. Detailed design and approvals
4. Procurement and tender
5. Construction and quality control
6. Handover, commissioning and operation

## Key stakeholders

- Developers and investors
- Architects and engineers
- Contractors and subcontractors
- Consultants (cost, legal, environmental)
- Planning authorities and building control
- Buyers, tenants and facility managers

## Key risks to track

- Cost overruns and changes in scope
- Delays in approvals or supply chains
- Design clashes between disciplines
- Regulatory and environmental compliance
- Market changes affecting value or demand
