# AEC RE Glossary

- **AEC**: Architecture, Engineering and Construction.
- **Feasibility study**: an assessment of whether a project is technically, financially and legally viable.
- **Site appraisal**: a review of a site's condition, constraints, access and planning position.
- **Schematic design**: early design stage that sets the layout, scale and main concepts of a building.
- **Planning permission**: approval from the local planning authority to develop a site.
- **Building control**: checks that construction meets building regulations.
- **Tender**: a formal process in which contractors submit priced bids for a project.
- **Procurement**: the process of selecting suppliers, contractors and consultants.
- **Bill of quantities**: a schedule of materials and work items used to price a project.
- **Practical completion**: the stage at which building work is finished and the building can be handed over.
- **Commissioning**: testing and setting up building systems so they operate as designed.
- **Defects liability period**: a period after handover during which the contractor must fix defects.
- **Yield**: the annual return on a property, usually shown as a percentage of its value or cost.
- **Asset management**: managing a property after completion to protect and grow its value.
